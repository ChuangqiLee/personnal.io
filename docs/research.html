03 / Human–AI interaction · Education

A multimodal teaching agent for K–12 learning

International Journal of Human-Computer Interaction

Second and corresponding author · Published 2 February 2026

Read paper ↗Publication record ↗

Contribution

Contributed to conceptualisation and methodology; coordinated project administration and resources; participated in investigation, validation, visualisation, and manuscript preparation.

System and evaluation

The system integrated LLaMA-based content generation, personalised voice cloning, and Wav2Lip for interactive teaching. A three-week field evaluation involved 300 students in Grades 5, 8, and 11, with outcomes varying by grade.

04 / Multimodal learning · Education

Multimodal classroom engagement assessment

International Journal of Human-Computer Interaction

First author · Project Lead, Henan Normal University · April 2022 – July 2025

Read paper ↗Publication record ↗

Project

Developed a system integrating facial expressions, body movements, and head pose to assess classroom engagement. Advisor: Prof. Dong Liu.

Methods and publication

Combined ResNet-based visual analysis, XGBoost classification, and Perspective-n-Point head-pose estimation; first author of the resulting 2024 journal publication.
